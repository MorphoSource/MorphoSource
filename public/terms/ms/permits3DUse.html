Term Name ms:permits3DUse op

Term IRI http://www.morphosource.org/terms/ms/permits3DUse
Label Permits 3D Use
Description

Whether Media may be 3D printed freely, may be 3D printed in limited ways, or if 3D printing is prohibited. Values must be from the 3D Printing Permission controlled vocabulary (http://www.morphosource.org/terms/ms/3DPrintingPermission).
Example 
`3DPrintingPermitted`, `3DPrintingLimited`, `3DPrintingProhibited`
Property Of
ms:Media | ms:Organization
Value Restriction mscv:3DPrintingPermission
