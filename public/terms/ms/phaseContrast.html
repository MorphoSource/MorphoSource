Term Name ms:phaseContrast dp

Term IRI http://www.morphosource.org/terms/ms/phaseContrast
Label Phase Contrast
Description

Whether phase contrast of X-Ray beam - in other words, modulating the beam between higher and lower energies to increase contrast between materials - is used or not. If present, value must be "Yes" or "No".
Example 
`Yes`, `No`
